## §2 THREAT MODEL
**Benchmark fishing** — probing candidates until one shows attractive headroom, or skipping a candidate that merely crashed.

## §3 DETERMINISTIC SHORTLIST CONSTRUCTION
Candidate names do not appear in the final shortlist until generated by this exact procedure. What is frozen here:

**3.1 EVIDENCE CUTOFF**: The cutoff timestamp is exactly determined by the machine-generated timestamp of formal operator acceptance of this RFC. Immediately upon acceptance, official source bytes, URLs, retrieval times, and hashes must be captured.

**3.2 CANDIDATE UNIVERSE FORMATION**: The candidate universe is explicitly and exhaustively restricted to the following exact finite benchmark identifiers and variants. 
1. `HumanEval`
2. `MBPP`
3. `SWE-bench Full`
4. `SWE-bench Lite`
5. `LiveCodeBench` (Tasks published up to 2024-08-01)
6. `APPS`
7. `DS-1000`
8. `MultiPL-E`
9. `ClassEval`
10. `BigCodeBench Full/Instruct`
11. `BigCodeBench Hard/Instruct`
12. `WebArena`

**3.3 ELIGIBILITY** (all required; failure = excluded, not ranked):
* **E1**: Deterministic, programmatic scoring.
* **E2**: Tasks and any required data obtainable reproducibly at a pinned exact version/commit (Git SHA or DOI).
* **E3**: License permits research use (non-commercial acceptable).
* **E4**: Documented evaluator interface.
* **E5**: $\ge$ 34 eligible tasks (12 admission + exactly 22 campaign).
* **E6**: $\ge$ 3 distinct structural task families with a source-authenticated task $\to$ family mapping. 
  * *Clarification*: Distinct semantic task families (e.g., data-science domains in DS-1000) that share the same function interface DO constitute valid structural families. The mapping must be explicitly audited from the benchmark's official repository metadata. Pure translations into other programming languages (e.g., MultiPL-E) are excluded.
* **E7**: No published result $\ge$ 0.95 on the full benchmark under its official harness by any frontier model.
  * *Exact Rule*: Authenticated capture of official repositories, papers, and official benchmark leaderboards is the primary source; PapersWithCode is secondary. 
  * *Frontier Mapping*: "Frontier model" is defined exactly as any model listed in the top 10 of the LMSYS Chatbot Arena Leaderboard snapshot captured at the evidence cutoff.
  * *Unknown Evidence Rule*: If a benchmark lacks published frontier results on its official page, it evaluates to "unknown" and conditionally passes this gate.
* **E8**: Single-generation compatibility. Interactive multi-turn agent environments (e.g., WebArena) are excluded.

**3.4 OVERLAPPING VARIANTS & CROSS-CANDIDATE EXCLUSION**: 
If multiple variants of the same benchmark (e.g. BigCodeBench Full vs Hard) are eligible, candidates must be processed in rank order. Before calculating the partition for a given candidate, any task overlapping with a task already probed in the admission set of a previously evaluated candidate is globally excluded from entering the partition.

**3.5 RANKING** (exact; E7 is the only permitted use of headroom evidence):
Score each eligible candidate on three integer axes, 0-2:
* **C (Compatibility)**: 2 = runs under sandboxed evaluator unmodified; 1 = requires documented local adapter; 0 = requires remote execution.
* **K (Cost)**: 2 = no external compute; 1 = laptop-feasible with storage/time cost; 0 = requires GCP escalation.
* **L (Licensing)**: 2 = permissive; 1 = non-commercial; 0 = restricted-but-permitted.

Rank by `(C, K, L)` descending. Final tie-break: ascending lexical order of the pinned benchmark identifier string. Maximum candidates carried forward: 4.

**3.6 STOPPING RULE**: The FIRST candidate passing all admission gates is selected. If all are validly rejected, the admission campaign terminates (negative outcome).

**3.7 SEALING**: The resulting shortlist is a separately SEALED artifact, containing exact URLs, pinned commits, versions, hashes, licenses, evaluator descriptions, and E7 results. It MUST be anchored by a trusted Git commit or a separately operator-held digest (not merely a writable `0o444` file).

## §4 FAULT HANDLING & ADMISSION SAFEGUARDS
**4.1 FAULT & RETRY TAXONOMY**: 
Advancement to the next candidate requires a VALID SCIENTIFIC REJECTION (§7). 
* Retry only objectively pre-response failures within the same logical call and schedule position. These are strictly limited to:
  - Connection refused, DNS failure, or TLS handshake failure proven before any request bytes were transmitted.
  - Subprocess spawn failure before `exec`.
  - HTTP 429/503 with zero response-body bytes received.
* Preserve call identity and cumulative accounting.
* Any other failure—including any post-transmission timeout, partial response, or ambiguous state—is non-retryable and invalidates the campaign.
* Evaluator retry may reuse only the exact authenticated response bytes; never resample the model.
* Intent without authenticated completion means unknown usage and invalidation.
* An unrecoverable infrastructure fault, timeout, crash, or ambiguity invalidates the candidate probe. Recover the SAME identity and complete it, or invalidate the ENTIRE admission campaign. Never skip to the next candidate due to a crash.

**4.2 EVALUATOR & CALL CAPS**:
* **Per-logical-call wall cap**: 120 seconds.
* **Evaluator limits**: 15 seconds CPU time and 15 seconds wall time per candidate evaluation.

**4.3 AGGREGATE CEILINGS**:
* **Per candidate**: $\le$ 36 logical calls, $\le$ 108 invocation attempts, $\le$ 100,000 output tokens, $\le$ 2 hours active model-invocation time.
* **Admission campaign total (4 candidates)**: $\le$ 144 logical calls, $\le$ 432 invocation attempts, $\le$ 400,000 output tokens, $\le$ 8 hours active model-invocation time.
Any breach fails closed and invalidates the admission campaign.

## §5 SAMPLING AND EFFECT THRESHOLD
**5.1 ADMISSION SAMPLING**: fixed $k=2$. A candidate whose pooled mean falls in the borderline band `[0.15, 0.95]` after $k=2$ receives an AUTOMATIC third sample for ALL 12 admission tasks (raising the admission calls to exactly 36).

**5.2 CAMPAIGN SAMPLING**: Fixed exactly at $k=2$ and $T=10$ trials. 

**5.3 PRACTICAL EFFECT THRESHOLD**: $\Delta_{practical} = 0.10$ normalized points, FROZEN HERE.

## §6 DETERMINISTIC PARTITIONING
**6.1 EXACT TASK COUNTS**: exactly 12 admission tasks (drawn first, permanently excluded from science). Exactly 22 remaining campaign tasks (4 optimization, 4 selection, 8 confirmatory, 6 holdout). Total 34 tasks required.

**6.2 FAMILY-BALANCED ALLOCATION ALGORITHM**:
The allocation targets exactly three structural families and publishes an exact quota matrix covering all three families in every tier.

1. **Family Selection**: Enumerate every ordered triple `(A,B,C)` of distinct source-authenticated structural families satisfying `count(A) ≥ 12`, `count(B) ≥ 11`, and `count(C) ≥ 11` (counting only eligible, non-overlapping tasks). Sort qualifying triples lexicographically by their UTF-8 identifier tuple and select the first. If none exists, the benchmark fails E5.
2. **Quota Matrix**:
   * Admission (12): Fam A: 4, Fam B: 4, Fam C: 4.
   * Optimization (4): Fam A: 2, Fam B: 1, Fam C: 1.
   * Selection (4): Fam A: 1, Fam B: 2, Fam C: 1.
   * Confirmatory (8): Fam A: 3, Fam B: 2, Fam C: 3.
   * Holdout (6): Fam A: 2, Fam B: 2, Fam C: 2.
3. **Hash Encoding**: Seed `S = SHA-256(UTF-8(benchmark_version_hash) || UTF-8("|rfc1/admission-partition/v2"))`.
4. **Task Ranking**: For every eligible task, compute `score = SHA-256(S || UTF-8("|") || UTF-8(task_id))`.
5. **Tie-Break**: If scores tie, sort by lexicographical `task_id`.
6. **Distribution**: Within each of the three selected families, sort tasks ascending by `score`. Pop tasks from the top of each family's sorted list sequentially to fill the exact quotas for Admission, Optimization, Selection, Confirmatory, and Holdout, in that exact order.

## §7 EXECUTABLE ADMISSION DECISION
**7.1 SCORE CALCULATION RULES**:
* **Task Mean**: The score for a single task is the arithmetic mean across its $k$ samples.
* **Final k Selection**: If the automatic third sample is triggered (§5.1), the final decision relies entirely on the $k=3$ task means. Otherwise, it relies on $k=2$.
* **Admission Mean**: The pooled mean is calculated with strictly **equal weight** across all 12 admission tasks, regardless of family size.
* **Validation**: Missing, non-finite, clipped, or out-of-range results have no score value, invalidate the probe before aggregation, and never enter any mean or scientific record.

**7.2 TERMINAL OUTCOMES**, evaluated in order on the admission mean:
* infrastructure fault or ambiguity $\to$ **invalid** (§4)
* pooled mean > 0.90 $\to$ **reject_saturated**
* pooled mean < 0.20 $\to$ **reject_floored**
* fewer than 4 admission tasks strictly in (0,1) ("Intermediate task" based on its rational task mean) $\to$ **reject_insufficient_resolution**
* otherwise $\to$ **pass**

## §9 ADAPTER / CORE BOUNDARY
