## 2. Scientific & Research Inspiration Attributions

RSILoop draws conceptual inspiration from published research but contains an
independent, clean-room implementation:

1. **Weco AI — AIDE²**
   - The bi-level prompt-optimization framing in Campaign 1 / Campaign 2 is inspired by Weco AI's research on [AIDE²](https://weco.ai/blog) (*Experimental evidence of consistent Level 1 recursive self-improvement in an AI research agent*).
   - No code, prompts, or proprietary artifacts from Weco AI are included in this repository, and RSILoop does not claim to reproduce or validate their findings.
2. **DiG-bench**
   - The experimental `discovery_probe/` active rule-discovery sidecar is conceptually inspired by [DiG-bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12593).
   - `discovery_probe/` is an independent, dependency-free Python implementation authored for RSILoop and contains no third-party benchmark code or data.
